The Eras Tour: Taylor Swift

Visual Identity - Poster - Promotional Material - Motion - Apparel - Packaging - Social Post

2024

I redesigned Taylor Swift's The Eras Tour with an emphasis for Swift's authorship and penmanship over her own songs and the distinct colors of every era.




Each sock showcases one line from its era's album.



The booklet follows Swift's setlist (without her surprise songs), as well as articles about her personified album eras and the records her world tour has broken.
